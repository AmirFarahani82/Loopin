# Loopin

## A habit-tracking app that helps you build your desired routines

### Loopin features:
- **Habit tracking** - track and complete your habits
- **Count & boolean habits** - two habit types
- **Streak freeze** - protect the streak of count-based habits for up to 3 days
- **GitHub-style heatmap** - visualize your progress
- **AI-powered insights** - based on your habit history
- **Analytics and charts** - view your consistency over time in detail

---

This project is built with **Next.js** and **TypeScript**, and uses **Supabase** for database and authentication
